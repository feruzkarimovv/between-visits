Between Visits

No symptom waits for the next visit, in any language, at any pace.

A voice agent calls cancer-trial patients in the days after a dose, in English or Spanish and at their pace. Fixed CTCAE rules grade the answers, the on-call nurse is paged for red flags, and the study team gets drafts with the patient's own words. On the sponsor side, Site Signal points a monitor at sites whose AE log disagrees with what their own patients reported.

The AI listens, rules grade, humans decide.

Team: @feruzkarimovv and the teammates listed on DevpostSynthetic data only · not a medical device · MIT

The problem

The patients trials struggle to reach between visits are the ones who don't speak English and the ones who need more time.

Non-English speakers

  • English-only staff, apps and forms miss them.
  • Clinical questions must not be machine-translated live.
  • Between Visits asks pre-written, approved questions in English or Spanish.

Patients who need more time

  • Fast voice bots and small screens fail older patients and people with low vision or literacy.
  • The extra-time variants speak slower, wait longer and read every answer back.
  • A plain phone-style conversation also catches what forms miss.
Source: Regeneron mentor conversation, Oct 10 (README § Why)2

Rosa, a synthetic patient

Rosa is 74, speaks Spanish and had her second dose two days ago. Her next visit is in three weeks.

What happens in the gap

  • Side effects start between visits and surface at the next one, from memory.
  • With an anti-PD-1 antibody, diarrhea can be the first sign of immune-related colitis; the example protocol treats 7 or more stools a day above baseline as grade 3.
  • Serious events must reach the sponsor within 24 hours of site awareness.

Clinician reports miss patient-reported symptoms

  • Across three randomized trials, physicians did not report 40.7–74.4% of the symptomatic toxicities patients reported (Di Maio 2015; secondary summary, UNVERIFIED against the full text).
  • NCI built PRO-CTCAE for this reason (NCI).
  • The patient stream is a reference, not the truth: patients report earlier and more often, clinicians judge attribution (Basch 2009; UNVERIFIED).
Sources: docs/10-site-signal.md §2 [1], [3], [5]; README § Why3

How it works

Plan → call → nurse → review → sponsor

PlanThe study team approves plain-language questions in English and Spanish, red flags from the drug class's patient guide, and calls on days 1, 3 and 7 after each dose.
→
CallThe agent says it is an AI, offers a person, and asks only the question the backend hands it, in the patient's language and pace.
→
NurseFixed CTCAE rules grade; a red flag pages the on-call nurse before the agent's next word, and re-pages until acknowledged.
→
ReviewDrafts carry the verbatim quote, language, second and source; AI translations are labeled. A person confirms each one.
→
SponsorThe investigator signs a serious-event report; the sponsor inbox shows the 24-hour clock, subject ID only.
Site Signal (sponsor side): each site's AE log is compared with what its own patients reported on these calls, relative to the other sites. A flag is a reason to look, not a finding.
Built with ElevenLabs and Claude (Anthropic API); FastAPI, Next.js, SQLite; numpy and scipy for Site Signal4

RuleHow it holdsProof

Readiness

Runs from a fresh clone, and every claim has a check

One command

make setup && make seed && make demo: demo mode replays a call through the real backend with no API keys. MIT licensed.

CI

Backend tests, data validation with a product-name guard, frontend typecheck and lint, a quickstart job, and a full-history secret scan.

Traceability

Every requirement maps to tagged tests and their latest result (validation/traceability.md).

Pre-registered

The Site Signal analysis plan was tagged sap-v1 before any output; the evaluation seed comes from a public beacon drawn after the tag.

Audit hash chain

Every human action is an append-only audit event; GET /api/audit/verify detects tampering inside the database.

Honest limits

WHAT_IT_GETS_WRONG.md and NOT_BUILT.md, one GitHub issue per item not built.

Pilot with one site

  • Real voice calls with measured speech-recognition error by language and pace.
  • More languages, professionally translated and clinically reviewed.
